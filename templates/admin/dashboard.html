What only an administrator can do

  • Survey the classrooms. Latitude, longitude and the 10 m radius. At this radius a coordinate typed off a map instead of measured in the room rejects the whole class. Classrooms →
  • Register the classroom networks. The egress IP that separates a student in the room from one at home. No other role can set this, and a location without one fails closed. Classrooms →
  • Hold the device register. Revoke a binding when a student changes phone or leaves. Devices →
  • Review refused attempts across all classes. A teacher sees only their own session; patterns spanning subjects are visible only here. Attempts →
  • Own the accounts and the timetable. Students, teachers, departments, classes and subjects. Students →
  • Report and export. Daily and per-student attendance, with CSV export. Reports →
